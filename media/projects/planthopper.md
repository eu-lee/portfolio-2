PlantHopper is a smart watering turret that gives every plant exactly the care it needs, individually. A CAD-designed 2-DOF turret uses AprilTag computer vision to locate each plant, then PID alignment and trigonometry to aim a tunable water gun for precise watering driven by soil moisture data. Manual and automated intervals for watering can be accessed on a dashboard, with an AI analysis on the health of the plant.
